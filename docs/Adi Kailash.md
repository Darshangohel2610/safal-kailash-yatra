## DAY 01

## KATHGODAM → PITHORAGARH

Approx. Travel Time: 7–8 hrs

Your spiritual Himalayan journey begins from Kathgodam. Meet our representative/driver at Kathgodam Railway Station and proceed towards Pithoragarh.

En route, visit some of Kumaon’s most revered spiritual destinations:

- Kainchi Dham — Neem Karoli Baba Ashram, a peaceful spiritual destination surrounded by Himalayan hills.

- Golu Devta Temple, Ghorakhal — Seek blessings at the famous temple dedicated to Golu Devta, the God of Justice.

- Jageshwar Dham — Ancient Shiva temple complex amid deodar forests; offer prayers and explore the complex.

After sightseeing, continue to Pithoragarh, gateway to the eastern Kumaon Himalayas.

Evening: Arrival and hotel check-in. Overnight Stay: Pithoragarh. Meals: Dinner.


## DAY 02

## PITHORAGARH → VYAS VALLEY → NABI / NAPALCHU

Approx. Travel Time: 7–8 hrs

After breakfast, begin the scenic journey towards the high Himalayan region of Vyas Valley. The route follows the beautiful Kali River, part of the India–Nepal border.

Along the way, enjoy spectacular views of :

- 🏔️ Himalayan mountain ranges

- Indo-Nepal border region

- Kali River

- Himalayan valleys and forests

- 🏘️ Remote mountain villages.

The journey becomes an important part of the Yatra, with dramatic mountain

roads and breathtaking scenery. Arrive at Nabi/Napalchu Village and check into your traditional Himalayan

homestay.

Evening: Rest and acclimatization. Overnight Stay: Nabi/Napalchu Homestay. Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.


## ADI KAILASH DARSHAN & CHARAN SPARSH

Approx. Dr ive: 3 hrs + local excursion/trek as appl icable

Today is one of the most spiritually significant days of your Yatra. After an early breakfast, proceed towards sacred Adi Kailash, also known as Chhota Kailash.

🕉️ Adi Kailash — Sacred peak associated with Lord Shiva; prayer and meditation amid magnificent Himalayan surroundings.

Parvati Sarovar — High-altitude lake associated with Goddess Parvati.

Pandav Kutir — Spiritually significant location associated with the Pandavas.

Bhim Ki Kheti — Himalayan site connected with local mythology and the Pandavas. Gauri Kund — Sacred water body associated with Goddess Parvati.

After Darshan and sightseeing, return towards Nabi/Napalchu. Evening: Rest at the homestay.

Overnight Stay: Nabi/Napalchu Homestay. Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.

Note: Vehicle access, trek/walking distance and sightseeing sequence may vary with local administration, road conditions and weather.


DAY 04

## OM PARVAT DARSHAN → DHARCHULA

Approx. Travel Time: 6–7 hrs

## Places Covered:

🏔️ Kalapani — Historically and geographically significant Himalayan region associated with the Kali River.

- 🕉️ Vyas Gufa — Sacred cave associated with Maharishi Ved Vyas and regional spiritual traditions.

🏔️ Nag Parvat — Majestic mountain scenery surrounding the region.

ॐ Om Parvat Darshan — Naturally formed snow pattern resembling the sacred symbol ॐ (Om).

Spend peaceful moments taking in the extraordinary view before the return journey. Proceed towards Dharchula.

Evening: Hotel check-in and rest. Overnight Stay: Dharchula. Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.

Wake up early and get ready for another spiritually memorable day. After breakfast, proceed towards Om Parvat through the spectacular landscapes of Vyas Valley.


DAY 05

## DHARCHULA → PATAL BHUVNESHWAR

Approx. Travel Time: 6 hrs

After breakfast, check out and begin your journey towards Patal Bhuvneshwar.

Enjoy changing Kumaon landscapes, winding mountain roads and picturesque villages.

Upon arrival, visit the famous Patal Bhuvneshwar Cave Temple.

## 🕉️ Patal Bhuvneshwar:

Patal Bhuvneshwar is a fascinating limestone cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and is deeply associated with Hindu mythology.

Descend into the naturally formed underground cave and experience its unique formations and spiritual atmosphere.

The cave is traditionally believed to contain representations of several Hindu deities and sacred sites.

After Darshan, return to the hotel/homestay and relax.

Overnight Stay: Patal Bhuvneshwar.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.


DAY 06

## PATAL BHUVNESHWAR → KATHGODAM

Approx. Travel Time: 8 hrs

After an early breakfast, check out and begin your return journey towards Kathgodam.

Enjoy the final views of the beautiful Kumaon Himalayas as the journey gradually takes you towards the plains.

Upon reaching Kathgodam, you will be dropped at Kathgodam Railway Station for your onward journey.

## YATRA ENDS

With the blessings of Lord Shiva, Adi Kailash and Om Parvat, your unforgettable Himalayan spiritual journey comes to an end.

Meals: Breakfast.
